### Date
Dec 31, 2026

### Bug Fixes

* Fixed [Wiegand.SetConfig]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#setconfig) applying the wrong format to CSN cards. When [WiegandConfig.CSNFormat]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#WiegandConfig) used a __formatID__ that none of __formats__ or __slaveFormats__ used, the device was pointed at the preceding format slot. A __CSNFormat__ that reuses a __formatID__ was not affected.
* Fixed [Wiegand.GetConfig]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#getconfig) omitting the 15th Wiegand format of a device.
* Fixed the device gateway terminating when [Wiegand.SetConfig]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#setconfig) received __formats__ holding the same __formatID__ more than 15 times. Such a request is now rejected as an invalid argument.

### New Features and Improvements

* XPass 2 V2 is supported, a hardware revision of XPass 2 with a new CPU and memory: up to 200,000 users, PIN on the keypad model and SEOS on the all-card model. Device types [XPASS2_V2 and XPASS2_KEYPAD_V2]({{'/api/device/' | relative_url}}#Type).
  - XP2-MDPB-V2, XP2-MAPB-V2, XP2-GDPB-V2 (XPASS2_V2)
  - XP2-GKDPB-V2 (XPASS2_KEYPAD_V2)
* Added the auth fail lockout, a protection against brute-force attacks on the PIN. The device counts the PIN failures within a time window and blocks PIN input for an escalating lockout time, with separate counters for access authentication and for administrator / intrusion authentication. Configure it with [AuthConfig.authFailLockoutConfig]({{'/api/auth/' | relative_url}}#AuthFailLockoutConfig), read the counters with [Auth.GetAuthFailStatus]({{'/api/auth/' | relative_url}}#getauthfailstatus) or [Auth.GetAllAuthFailStatus]({{'/api/auth/' | relative_url}}#getallauthfailstatus) and end a lockout early with [Auth.ReleaseAuthFailLockout]({{'/api/auth/' | relative_url}}#releaseauthfaillockout) or [Auth.ReleaseAllAuthFailLockout]({{'/api/auth/' | relative_url}}#releaseallauthfaillockout). Supported devices report [DeviceCapability.authFailLockoutSupported]({{'/api/device/' | relative_url}}#DeviceCapability). The device logs __BS2_EVENT_DEVICE_AUTHFAIL_LOCKOUT__ (0x4505), __BS2_EVENT_DEVICE_AUTHFAIL_LOCKOUT_RESET__ (0x4506) and __BS2_EVENT_DEVICE_LOCKOUT_REQUEST_BY_OPERATOR__ (0x4507); see [the Event API]({{'/api/event/' | relative_url}}).
* Added the custom FeliCa smart card configuration: [Card.GetCustomFelicaConfig]({{'/api/card/' | relative_url}}#GetCustomFelicaConfig), [SetCustomFelicaConfig]({{'/api/card/' | relative_url}}#SetCustomFelicaConfig) and [SetCustomFelicaConfigMulti]({{'/api/card/' | relative_url}}#SetCustomFelicaConfigMulti) with [CustomFelicaConfig]({{'/api/card/' | relative_url}}#CustomFelicaConfig). Supported devices report [DeviceCapability.customSmartCardFelicaSupported]({{'/api/device/' | relative_url}}#DeviceCapability); enable __CARD_OPERATION_MASK_CUSTOM_FELICA__ in [SystemConfig.useCardOperationMask]({{'/api/system/' | relative_url}}#SystemConfig). The device never returns the keys, so they must be re-entered on every set.
* Added [DisplayConfig.standbyType]({{'/api/display/' | relative_url}}#DisplayConfig) to choose what the screen shows after the backlight timeout: the home screen, the slide-image screen saver, or a video. See [StandbyType]({{'/api/display/' | relative_url}}#StandbyType). __useScreenSaver__ is deprecated in its favour.
* [Wiegand.SetConfig]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#setconfig) now rejects a __formatID__ used twice within __formats__ or within __slaveFormats__. The device looks its format slots up by ID, so the duplicate was silently dropped before. Reusing an ID across __formats__, __slaveFormats__ and __CSNFormat__ is still supported and shares one slot.
* Documented that [WiegandConfig.CSNFormat]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#WiegandConfig) takes effect only while [CardConfig.useWiegandFormat]({{'/api/card/' | relative_url}}#CardConfig) is enabled. See [the Wiegand API]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}) and [the Card API]({{'/api/card/' | relative_url}}).
* Clarified which of [WiegandConfig.formats and WiegandConfig.slaveFormats]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}#WiegandConfig) applies to which path. The signals arriving at the Wiegand input port of a device are decoded with __slaveFormats__, while __formats__ covers the cards it reads with its own reader, so configuring Wiegand input means filling in __slaveFormats__. The previous wording only said "applied to the device itself" and "applied to its slave devices", which was easy to read the other way around.
* Corrected the maximum number of Wiegand formats per device from 16 to 15 in [the Wiegand API]({{'/api/wiegand/' | relative_url}}) and in __Wiegand.MAX_WIEGAND_FORMATS__. The device has always had 15 format slots.
